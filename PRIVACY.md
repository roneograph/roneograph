# Privacy policy

Roneograph does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No servers of ours.** Roneograph has no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no account. The app connects to the network only to deliver a scan to the archive server you configured, when you tap Archive.
- **On your iPhone only.** Photos, pages and PDFs are processed and kept on your iPhone, in the app's folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone › Roneograph. Scans are deleted when you delete them, or after the number of days chosen in Settings (7 by default) unless you choose to keep them forever.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only to capture the pages you scan, while the scanner is open.
- **Text recognition.** The text of the pages is read on your iPhone, to suggest a file name, to find the details of the document (category, issuer, date, reference number) and to add an invisible text layer to the PDF so it can be searched. The text itself is kept only inside the PDF; the details are kept with the scan.
- **Corrections.** The names, issuers and categories you correct are kept on your iPhone to be reused for the next documents. To recognize your own name and address, and never take them for the issuer, the app keeps fingerprints of the lines that come back on your documents, not their text. Settings › Memory shows what is remembered for each issuer, and forgets one issuer or all of it.
- **Sending.** A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you send it: with Email or Share, to the recipient or app you pick, or with Archive, to the archive server you set up in Settings. The archive server receives only the PDF, its name and, when known, the document date. The default email address, the archive server address and its credentials stay on your iPhone; credentials are kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only.

The app is open source under the MIT license, so all of this can be checked in its source code.
