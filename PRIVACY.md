# Privacy policy

Roneograph does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No network.** The app never connects to the internet: no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no account.
- **On your iPhone only.** Photos, pages and PDFs are processed and kept on your iPhone, in the app's folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone › Roneograph. Scans are deleted after the number of days chosen in Settings (7 by default), or when you delete them.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only to capture the pages you scan, while the scanner is open.
- **Text recognition.** The text of the first page is read on your iPhone to suggest a file name. It is not stored and not added to the PDF.
- **Sending.** A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you send it with Email or Share, to the recipient or app you pick. The default email address you may enter in Settings stays on your iPhone.

The app is open source under the MIT license, so all of this can be checked in its source code.
