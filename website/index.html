Roneograph

Paperwork to searchable PDF/A

A free, open source iPhone scanner for administrative paper: letters, invoices, contracts, payslips, statements. Point the camera at a page and get a clean, small, searchable PDF/A, ready to archive or send.

Download on the App Store Source on GitHub

iPhone with iOS 26. Offline, no ads, no account, no tracking.

Somewhere in your home, there is a drawer full of paper. Payslips, tax notices, the lease, bank statements, a contract you signed years ago and never read again. Roneograph exists to empty that drawer.

The best scan of a sheet of paper

Roneograph gives back the sheet itself: flat, upright, at its real paper size, white where the paper is white and black where the ink is black. Shadows, perspective, fingers and the desk go away; a faint line of print, a pencil note, a stamp or a signature always stay.

Small files, without losing a single word

Compression may make a page a little less pretty; it must never make it say something else. Roneograph never uses a compression that can swap one character for another.

Searchable

Every PDF carries the text of its pages, recognized on your iPhone and invisible under the image. Files, Mail, Spotlight on a Mac and your archive tools can find it, and a screen reader can read it aloud.

Built to last

Every file is PDF/A, the ISO standard for long-term archiving. No proprietary format, no database: your archive is plain PDF files that will look the same decades from now.

Yours

Administrative paper is the most private paper you own. Roneograph works on your phone, offline, with no account and no tracking. Nothing leaves the phone unless you send it: to your own Paperless-ngx server, or to any other app with Share.
