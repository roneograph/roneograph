# The Roneograph manifesto

Somewhere in your home, there is a drawer full of paper. Payslips, tax notices, the lease, bank statements, a contract you signed years ago and never read again. One day, someone asks for one of them, and you spend the evening looking for it.

Roneograph exists to empty that drawer. It turns administrative paper into PDF archives: letters, invoices, contracts, payslips, statements, forms, receipts. Mostly black text on white paper, with the occasional colored logo, stamp, or signature.

## The best scan of a sheet of paper

Roneograph gives back the sheet itself: flat, upright, at its real paper size, white where the paper is white and black where the ink is black.

It removes what comes from the photograph and keeps what is on the paper. Shadows, perspective, fingers, and the desk go away; so do hole punches, staples, and the text showing through from the back. A faint line of print, a pencil note in the margin, a stamp, or a signature always stay. When Roneograph cannot tell the difference, it keeps the mark.

Pages are gray; color is kept only where it is on the page.

## Small files, without losing a single word

Roneograph makes the smallest PDFs it can without losing information. Compression may make a page a little less pretty; it must never make it say something else. Roneograph never uses a compression that can swap one character for another, at any size setting, and the smallest setting still has to be readable by a human and by OCR.

## Searchable

Every PDF carries the text of its pages, recognized on your phone and invisible under the image, so the page looks exactly like the paper. You find a document with Spotlight, in Files, in your mail, or in whatever tool holds your archive, and a screen reader can read it aloud.

## Built to last

All PDFs are PDF/A, the ISO standard for long-term archiving. A PDF/A file contains everything needed to display it and depends on nothing outside of it, so it will look the same in any PDF reader decades from now. There is no proprietary format and no database: your archive is plain PDF files.

## Yours

Administrative paper is the most private paper you own: your income, your address, your health, your debts. Roneograph works on your phone, offline, with no account and no tracking. Nothing leaves the phone unless you send it. It is free and Open-Source (MIT).

## How we decide

A feature belongs in Roneograph if it makes the archive of an administrative document more faithful, smaller, or easier to find.
